❓ How to Play

Follow these simple steps to build a healthier plate.

  • 🍽️ Pick a food from the tray.
  • 🎯 Drag it to the matching plate section.
  • 🥦 Try to include vegetables, fruits, grains and protein.
  • 🚫 Avoid junk food — it lowers your score and health meter.
  • 🔥 Make 3 correct choices in a row for a combo bonus.
  • ⏱️ Finish quickly to earn a speed bonus.

    🌍 SDG 3 — Good Health & Well-Being

    Plate Up! connects healthy food choices with Sustainable Development Goal 3.

    🎯 What is SDG 3?

    SDG 3 aims to ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for people of all ages.

    🥗 Why nutrition matters

    A balanced diet supports growth, energy and long-term health. The game turns these ideas into simple decisions students can practice.

    🧠 Learn while playing

    The game combines food sorting, healthy-choice feedback, a quiz, daily challenges and achievements.

    💡 Remember

    This is an educational game, not medical advice or a diagnostic tool.
